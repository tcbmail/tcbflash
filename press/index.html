About Ben

Ben Hornung is the founder and CEO of FlashForce. He began his career in computing as a network administrator and software test engineer, including work at Citrix. He later ran a Gulf Coast construction business, helped rebuild apartments after Hurricane Katrina, and sold used auto parts online through Junkyarder. Now based in Missouri, he is developing AI employees for the kinds of small businesses he knows firsthand.

About FlashForce

FlashForce is developing software that lets a manager give an AI employee an ongoing job, teach it how the business works, and decide what it can do on its own. Visit FlashForce.
